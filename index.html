About Me

Hi! I am a 21 year old transfer student at the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute studying Computer Systems Engineering with a concentration in Robotics and Automation. Throughout my education at two rigorous engineering institutions, I have developed experience with several programming languages and technologies, including C++, C, Python, Javascript, React, and CSS, as well as modeling software such as SolidWorks, PTC Creo, Siemens NX, and ROS2. Through my educational and extracurricular projects, I have gained a deep understanding of engineering processes, project management, problem solving, and time management. I transferred to RPI in the Fall of 2023 from the Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology, where I ran NCAA Track and Field and was inducted into the Alpha Lambda Delta Honor Society, earning a 3.8 GPA before transferring to RPI. My experience at Rose-Hulman as a student athlete was transformative, and greatly solidified effective time-management habits that I continued with my education at RPI. This has allowed me to hold several leadership positions, such as the social media manager and assistant recruitment chair for the Lambda Chi Alpha Fraternity, and as the community outreach coordinator for the RPI Robotics Club.
